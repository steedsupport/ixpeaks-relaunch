# IXPEAKS emblem-to-wordmark spacing standard

## Core measurement

Let **H** equal the visible height of the IXPEAKS emblem.

The vertical clear gap **G** between the emblem and the `IXPEAKS` wordmark is:

**G = 0.20 × H**

Measure the gap from the emblem's lowest visible point to the top cap-height edge of the wordmark, perpendicular to the wordmark baseline. Production tolerance is **±0.02 × H**.

Examples:

- 50 px emblem: 10 px gap
- 100 px emblem: 20 px gap
- 25 mm emblem: 5 mm gap

## Application rules

- Use a supplied vertical-lockup SVG as one grouped object. Do not position the emblem and wordmark separately.
- Scale, rotate, skew, or perspective-map the entire lockup together. The gap must scale with the lockup.
- On angled product panels, measure the gap perpendicular to the transformed wordmark baseline—not vertically against the full photograph.
- For dark products, use the split lockup: Flame emblem, white `IX`, and Flame `PEAKS`.
- Preserve the approved type size, letter spacing, horizontal centering, and exact `IXPEAKS` spelling.
- Do not add outlines, shadows, backing shapes, bevels, or sticker-like effects.
- For fabric applications, allow the material weave, folds, highlights, and shadows to continue naturally through the printed artwork.

## Approved master lockups

- `ixpeaks-lockup-vertical-split.svg` — preferred for charcoal and black products
- `ixpeaks-lockup-vertical-flame.svg` — single-color Flame version
- `ixpeaks-lockup-vertical-white.svg` — single-color reverse version

All three masters encode the same **0.20H** emblem-to-wordmark gap.
