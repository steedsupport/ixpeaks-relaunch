# IXPEAKS apparel logo-placement research and proposed standard

Status: research draft for approval. No product images were changed while preparing this document.

## 1. What Columbia and The North Face consistently do

The clearest recurring convention is a compact primary mark on the **wearer's left chest**. In a straight-on product photograph, that is the **viewer's right**. The position is adjusted to the garment's construction rather than copied as a fixed photo coordinate.

- The North Face specifies a left-chest logo across insulated jackets, fleece, tees, active tops, and children's outerwear. Depending on material, it uses embroidery, heat transfer, reflective heat transfer, or water-based screen printing.
- On several jackets, The North Face adds a secondary mark at the back-right shoulder. That is a distinctive TNF convention and is not recommended for the IXPEAKS core system.
- The North Face commonly places pants branding on the wearer's left thigh or on a left-thigh pocket. Some specialized rain pants use a position above the right knee, showing that construction and function can override the default.
- Columbia also uses compact chest branding. Columbia customer support states that the logo on its Arcadia II rain jacket is approximately 2.5 inches long. Columbia product descriptions also identify chest logos and material-specific applications such as silicone.

## 2. IXPEAKS placement system

### Side convention

- **Default apparel position: wearer's left.**
- On a front-facing flat lay or mannequin image, this is the **right side of the image**.
- Never describe placement only as "left" or "right." Every production instruction must say both: `wearer's left / viewer's right`.

### Construction-first rule

The logo must sit inside one visually coherent garment panel. It must not cross a zipper, seam, baffle, pocket opening, drawcord, placket, or strong fold. Its baseline and vertical axis should follow the local panel perspective. The mark is optically centered in the usable panel, not mathematically centered in the whole photograph.

This rule takes priority over the default left-chest convention. If the wearer's left chest is occupied by a prominent pocket zipper, chest pocket, placket, badge panel, or other hardware, move the mark to the wearer's right chest (viewer-left) when that side provides the cleaner uninterrupted panel. The objective is the same visual balance used by Columbia and The North Face, not mechanical adherence to one side.

### Recommended size

- Adult outerwear and fleece: **55–65 mm overall lockup width**; default 60 mm.
- Adult tees, hoodies, and lightweight tops: **50–60 mm overall lockup width**; default 55 mm.
- Youth outerwear and tops: **38–48 mm overall lockup width**; default 42 mm.
- Pants and shorts: **30–42 mm emblem width**. Use the emblem alone unless the panel clearly supports a readable lockup.
- Use these as physical production targets. In mockups, scale them relative to the garment, not relative to the image canvas.

### Placement by garment type

| Garment | IXPEAKS default | Construction adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Full-zip jacket / shell | Wearer's left chest, viewer's right | Center in the clear chest panel between center zip and armhole. If that side contains a prominent chest-pocket zipper or hardware, use the clean opposite chest panel instead. |
| Pullover / anorak | Wearer's left chest, viewer's right | Place above the horizontal pouch or chest seam. Do not center the mark under the neck unless the design is intentionally a central badge. |
| Puffer / quilted jacket | Wearer's left chest, viewer's right | Center within a single baffle; never bridge a quilting seam. |
| Fleece | Wearer's left chest, viewer's right | Center within a stable knit panel and avoid pile distortion or zipper edges. |
| Tee / hoodie / base layer | Wearer's left chest, viewer's right | Small chest mark; do not use the current centered-under-collar treatment for core product imagery. |
| Pants / joggers | Wearer's left upper thigh, viewer's right | Place below the hand-pocket opening or within a thigh-pocket panel; avoid knee articulation and cargo-pocket seams. |
| Shorts | Wearer's left lower thigh, viewer's right | Place above the hem and away from the pocket opening; align to the leg panel. |
| Bib / snowpant | Wearer's left bib/chest panel, viewer's right | Center in the uninterrupted bib panel below the strap attachment and away from the center zipper. |

### Mark choice

- Use the **emblem alone** for compact clothing placements. This follows the restraint seen on technical outdoor apparel and avoids an oversized badge effect.
- Use the **vertical emblem-plus-IXPEAKS lockup** only where there is enough uninterrupted space and the name remains legible at real production size.
- If the vertical lockup is used, preserve the approved `0.20H` emblem-to-wordmark gap from `LOCKUP-STANDARD.md`.
- On charcoal/black fabric, use the split lockup only when the wordmark is necessary: Flame emblem, white `IX`, Flame `PEAKS`.

## 3. Material execution

| Surface | Recommended appearance |
|---|---|
| Softshell / woven shell | Matte heat transfer or low-profile embroidery; fabric grain and shading must remain visible. |
| Waterproof shell / anorak | Matte or reflective heat transfer; no bevel, outline, backing rectangle, or sticker edge. |
| Puffer | Embroidery or low-profile transfer centered within one baffle; follow baffle curvature. |
| Fleece | Embroidery with slight thread texture; no perfectly flat digital fill. |
| Cotton tee / hoodie | Water-based screen print or fine embroidery; let the knit texture and folds pass naturally through the mark. |
| Pants / shorts | Low-profile heat transfer or embroidery, selected to match the fabric weight and stretch. |

## 4. Mockup instructions for Hermes

1. Start from a clean, unbranded product image whenever available.
2. Identify the wearer's left side before placing anything; on a front view this is the viewer's right.
3. Identify the usable panel bounded by seams, zipper, armhole, baffle, pocket, and folds. If the default left-chest panel is occupied by hardware, use the clean right-chest panel.
4. Select emblem-only by default. Use the full vertical lockup only when explicitly assigned.
5. Perspective-map the complete artwork to the local panel. Do not rotate the emblem and wordmark independently.
6. Blend the artwork into the material: preserve weave, wrinkles, highlights, shadows, and panel curvature through the mark.
7. Keep edges clean but not digitally perfect; the application should read as embroidery, transfer, or print appropriate to that fabric.
8. Do not invent extra marks. Core apparel uses one primary front mark.
9. Compare the result against a garment-specific reference, not against another asset with different seams or proportions.
10. Reject the image if the mark reads as a sticker, floats across a seam, sits on the wrong side, or ignores the panel angle.

## 5. QA checklist

- [ ] Wearer's left / viewer's right is explicitly verified.
- [ ] If the default side contains prominent hardware, the cleaner opposite panel is used.
- [ ] Mark is inside a single uninterrupted panel.
- [ ] Mark does not cross a seam, zip, pocket opening, baffle, or strong fold.
- [ ] Scale matches the garment class and does not dominate the product.
- [ ] Baseline and vertical axis follow the panel perspective.
- [ ] Material texture, light, and shadow continue through the mark.
- [ ] Correct application method is visible: embroidery, print, or transfer.
- [ ] Correct approved colourway is used for the fabric colour.
- [ ] If the full lockup is used, the `0.20H` gap is preserved.
- [ ] Spelling is exactly `IXPEAKS`.
- [ ] Only one primary front mark is present unless a secondary placement is specifically approved.

## 6. Decision for the current catalog

The existing clothing sources should not be used as placement references. Several show a mark on the viewer's left (the wearer's right), while some folded tops place the mark centrally under the collar. Those positions conflict with the recommended IXPEAKS system.

The next production pass should therefore use the clean garment construction as the reference and rebuild every clothing logo placement from this standard. The six approved test files remain preserved as visual tests and are not to be edited.
