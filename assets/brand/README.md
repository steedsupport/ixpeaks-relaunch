## Usage

- Use Flame on light, bone, grey, and neutral surfaces.
- Use white on charcoal, black, deep grey, and dark photographic areas.
- Use Charcoal on Flame orange, white, and very light surfaces.
- Use one mark per product unless an approved collection badge is also required.
- Use the compact vertical lockup where the full IXPEAKS name is required on bottles, packs, and equipment.
- Keep the emblem-to-wordmark gap at exactly `0.20 × emblem height` (`0.20H`), with a production tolerance of `±0.02H`.
- Always transform the vertical lockup as one grouped object; never position the emblem and wordmark independently.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio; never stretch, skew, rotate, outline, or add effects.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Recommended minimum height: 24 px digitally, 12 mm for print, and 15 mm for embroidery.

The faceted primary mark is reserved for larger digital and campaign applications. Use these flat marks for footwear, chest branding, bottles, packs, caps, labels, and other small-format applications.
